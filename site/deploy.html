Stage 3 · deploy-mvx.sh

Deploy and check

Push the image into the VM, launch the gateway, and prove each layer works: WAN, OpenSync to its cloud, then the extender topology. Every check prints PASS or FAIL with the evidence, and leaves a status file in the VM.

./deploy-mvx.sh all        # push + launch + check + opensync (Plume)

push stages the image and the pinned meta-lxd in the VM · launch starts mv3 with meta-lxd's mv.sh: WAN on br-wan101, LAN port on br-lan201, three radios from the pool · check is the WAN gate · opensync enables SON and connects to the cloud.

./deploy-mvx.sh mesh       # opensync --cloud local, pod-1..3, 2 clients each, topology check

Switches mv3 to local-noc, pushes the extender image once, launches pod-1..N and checks each onboards, launches pod-N-wc1..M and checks each reaches the internet, then checks the whole topology. MVX_PODS (3) and MVX_POD_CLIENTS (2) set the size.

./deploy-mvx.sh push [--image PATH]
./deploy-mvx.sh launch | check | status | shell
./deploy-mvx.sh opensync --cloud plume      # or --cloud local
./deploy-mvx.sh pod pod-2                   # one extender
./deploy-mvx.sh client pod-2-wc1 pod-2      # one client on it
./deploy-mvx.sh noc nodes | tables mv3 | dump mv3 <table> | log mv3 [N] | transact mv3 '<op>'

The last run

Recorded from the lab by build-docs.sh capture (the status files in /var/lib/opensync-lab). Open a check for its lines.

Cloud: Plume or local-noc

opensync --cloud plume points SONURL at the Plume redirector (TLS, theta dev certificates); --cloud local at local-noc (plain TCP). A switch restarts OpenSync on mv3 while its WAN is already up; this image's cm then commits to IPv6, which this lab has no upstream for. The check handles that (IPv6 marked blocked on the uplink, cm restarted), as it handles two other known image issues: no NOC certificates selected, and dnsmasq failing with SON on. All are listed with their proper fixes in the reference.
